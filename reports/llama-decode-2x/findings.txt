FALSIFIED OR INSUFFICIENT HYPOTHESES
1. Independent accumulation chains: the Q6_K microbenchmark improved from
   212 to 182 us with two chains, while Q4_K stayed near 284 us. Full-model GPU
   time remained 27.08 ms versus 27.02 ms original. Not a model decode win.
2. Lossless Q6_K scale repacking: the microbenchmark reached 180 us, but the
   full model remained 27.05 ms. Precision of the stored scales is widened
   losslessly; packed weight bits are unchanged. LAB_QLAYOUT also tests Q4_K.
3. Submission splitting: one large submission removed chunk waits but yielded
   27.12 ms GPU and 44.95 ms wall. It was not the main bottleneck. Semantic
   command telemetry found ZERO explicit CopyBuffer commands in this decode
   plan; shader-based scatter/copy work is still present.
4. Disabling wavefront groups: launches increased 1270 -> 1450 and GPU time
   worsened to 27.58 ms. No win.
5. Quantized scale/offset factoring: after fixing the address proof, 32 terms
   formed four scale groups. Q4_K still took 278 us and Q6_K 244 us. CPU checks
   passed. The first broken-proof experiment formed 32 groups and was far slower.
   This unsuccessful prototype is removed from compiled code and preserved in
   factoring-experiment.patch for review. It was never accepted on full-model
   accuracy or enabled by default.
6. Ten SGEMV schedules per format: no substantial Q4_K gain. Some explicit pins
   fell back to fold kernels; those records are marked invalid comparisons.
   Q6_K samples varied substantially, so isolated single-run wins were not
   promoted. Model testing the one-subgroup schedule showed no decode win.
7. Compiler fusion search DOES exhaust its node budget after two rounds,
   despite allowing ten rounds. Four times the budget reaches three rounds
   and reduces launches from 1270 to 1000, but GPU time remains 28.04 ms and
   wall grows to 35.91 ms; cold extraction is approximately 19 seconds.
   Fewer dispatches alone are not a performance result. Do not enable scale 4
   as an optimization.
