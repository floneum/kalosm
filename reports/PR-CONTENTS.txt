This directory publishes selected tools, summaries, and verification evidence
for the kernel visualizer and WebGPU Llama decode improvements.

Start with llama-decode-2x/default-inference.txt for the default-path change
and its current validation status. Historical opt-in measurements are in
llama-decode-2x/verified-short-decode.txt. Exact final timing samples,
configuration/binary provenance, full-logit comparison summaries, and resource
samples are under llama-decode-2x/webgpu-memory-bounded/prepared-combined/.

The complete 3.4 GiB local experiment archive is not part of the PR. Historical
findings refer to local artifacts that may not be published here. Model files,
executables, raw .f32 reference/candidate vectors, generated shader dumps, and
rejected native-backend prototypes are excluded. Source and compact validation
results are retained; the excluded experiments are not build dependencies.

The correctness tool accepts --reference for a separately captured baseline.
Raw reference vectors must be generated locally before checking a fresh clone;
see the capture recipe in llama-decode-2x/verified-short-decode.txt.
